# TruckBeez Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Last updated:** [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitchnow LLC ("Kitchnow," "we," "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the TruckBeez mobile app, website and related services (the "Services"). TruckBeez is built for professional truck drivers and the logistics businesses they work with.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**
- Account details: name, email, phone number, password or sign-in credentials.
- Truck profile: vehicle height, weight, length, axle count, trailer type, and hazmat class if you choose to enter it.
- Trip details: destinations, saved places, preferences.
- Reports and content: road hazards, closures, reviews, photos and comments you post.
- Support messages and survey answers.
- Fleet account details (for business customers): company name, billing contact, driver roster.

**Information collected automatically**
- Precise location: GPS location while you navigate or, if you are on a fleet plan, while you are on duty. With your permission, this can continue in the background during an active trip.
- Driving data: speed, heading, route followed, stops and arrival times.
- Device data: device model, OS version, app version, language, crash logs, IP address.
- Usage data: features used, screens viewed, settings.

**Information from others**
- Your fleet employer, if it adds you to a TruckBeez fleet account.
- App stores and payment providers (subscription status; we do not receive full card numbers).
- Map and routing partners (road and restriction data, not data about you).

## 2. How we use information

- Provide truck-specific routing, hazard alerts and arrival estimates.
- Show and improve shared road reports for the driver community.
- Provide fleet tracking and reports to your employer if you are on a fleet plan.
- Process subscriptions and send service messages.
- Keep the Services safe: detect fraud and abuse, and secure accounts.
- Analyze and improve the Services, mostly with aggregated or de-identified data.
- Send marketing emails if you agree; you can unsubscribe at any time.
- Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

## 3. Precise location

We treat precise location as sensitive information. We collect it only after you grant permission in the app and your device settings. You can turn it off at any time, but navigation will not work without it. We do not sell your location data or share it with data brokers or advertisers.

## 4. How we share information

- **Your fleet employer:** if you join a fleet account, the fleet sees your location, routes and trip reports while you are on duty or navigating for that fleet. Your employer is responsible for telling you how it uses this data. You can see when sharing is active in the app.
- **Other drivers:** road reports you post are shown to other users with a display name, not your contact details.
- **Service providers:** hosting, mapping and routing, analytics, customer support, email and payment providers who process data for us under contract.
- **Legal and safety:** to comply with law, valid legal process, or to protect the safety and rights of people.
- **Business transfers:** in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under this Policy's protections.
- **With your consent:** for any other purpose you agree to.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 5. Retention

We keep account information while your account is open. We keep detailed location trails for [90] days unless your fleet plan sets a different period, then delete or aggregate them. We keep billing records as required by law. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your data within [30] days, except where we must keep it.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- Change location permissions in your device settings.
- Pause fleet sharing with the off-duty toggle (your employer may set rules about this).
- Delete your account in the app under Settings > Account > Delete account.
- Opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link.

Depending on where you live (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to: know and access the personal information we hold; correct it; delete it; get a portable copy; opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising; and limit use of sensitive information. We honor Global Privacy Control signals. To make a request, email [PRIVACY EMAIL] or use the in-app form. We will verify your identity and respond within the time required by law (usually 45 days). You may appeal a denial by replying to our decision. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

**Fleet drivers:** if your employer's fleet account controls your data, we may refer your request to your employer and help it respond.

## 7. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and monitoring to protect information. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as required by law.

## 8. Children

TruckBeez is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has given us information.

## 9. International users

We operate in the United States and store data there. If you use TruckBeez outside the US, your information will be transferred to the US.

## 10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. For material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
